Paseo por Sevilla 

The sweet smell of orange blossom as you stroll along the banks of the Guadalquivir. The clip-clop of horse hooves and the sight of children playing in the streets at 10 PM, like there's no school tomorrow. Then that sun. Life must be good her.
Even in winter, Sevilla looks like summer.
