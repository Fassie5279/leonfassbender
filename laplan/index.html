The Warmth of Lapland

The  Nordic winter strips the landscape down to its quietest elements. The only sounds interrupting the snow are the muffled crunch of ice under your boots, the crackle of burning pine, and the distant hum of a snowmobile. Yet, beneath the freezing temperatures of Lapland, a quiet warmth endures.
