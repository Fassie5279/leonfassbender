Upcoming short film: "Non ci pensare mai" (No time without love, no love without time).

Leon Fassbender
I'm currently working on a short film called "Non ci pensare mai" (No time without love, no love without time). It's a film about love, grief, and the passage of time. For some reason that I cannot quite articulate, the concept of time is something that deeply interests me. In photography, this expresses itself as a desire to capture a moment that will never be exactly the same again, the way you experienced it. The very second you realize you are seeing something, it has already changed. Coincidentally (or maybe not, according to Carl Jung), last week I came across this quote by the Dutch poet and writer Cees Buddingh:
"Look around you, look around you. As the world is right now, you will never see it again."
— Cees Buddingh' (1918–1985)
 
This beautifully encapsulates my 'philosophy' when it comes to photography. I guess making a short film about time is just a natural extension of that, though I don't really know for sure. Anyway, for the setting of this film, I chose a remote mountain village in Abruzzo in the year 1970, which I think creates the perfect mood for a story like this. I hope to release it soon!
