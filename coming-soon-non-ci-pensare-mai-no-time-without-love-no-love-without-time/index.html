Coming Soon: "Non ci pensare mai" (No time without love. No love without time.) 

Leon Fassbender

I'm currently working on a short film called "Non ci pensare mai" (No time without love. No love without time). It's a short film about love, memory, and the relentless passage of time, played out in in the magical realism genre. For the set I chose a (fictional) remote Abruzzo mountain village in the year 1970, which I think sets the perfect mood for a film like this. I hope to release it soon.
