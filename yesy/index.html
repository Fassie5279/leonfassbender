An evening at Valle dei Templi, Agrigento

Leon Fassbender

As we arrive in the parking lot, there's dust everywhere. Although it's early august and Ferragosto has not yet started it feels like the whole of Italy has planned to come here today. There are cars everywhere, and -in typical Sicilian fashion-, hardly any road signage to tell you where to go, or even how to pay for the parking.
