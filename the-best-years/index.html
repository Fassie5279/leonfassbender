The Best Years - A poem (about time as a memory)

Leon Fassbender
The Best Years 
 
The best years come like a sigh of wind with blossom leaves, that blanket my street.

Like an October table with candlelight that warms my soul.

Like the look of the good, which looks at me, in love.

With the bridge and the city on the other side, where lights glow.

On the photograph of everything that must stay.

Like the nights of Osaka.
Or the attic.
The room of my heart that races like a bullet train.
Definite, on time.
 
All the time knowing—
The best years.

They went unnoticed.
